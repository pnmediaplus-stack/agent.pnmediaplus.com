# 054_MARKETING_ROOM_CHARTER_v1

**Classification:** `[SYSTEM ROOT]`  
**Status:** Charter for Marketing Room in the PN OS multi-department system  
**Purpose:** Define the scope, authority, boundaries, inputs, outputs, and operating rules for the Marketing Room

---

# 0. Charter Statement

Marketing Room exists to translate verified business truth into governed marketing execution without crossing billing, auth, or runtime boundaries.

It is a coordination room, not a truth factory.

---

# 1. Mission

Marketing Room exists to:

- convert approved product truth into marketing-ready artifacts
- maintain campaign orchestration
- preserve brand, claim, and proof discipline
- coordinate specialist outputs across content, growth, narrative, and distribution
- protect the system from overclaim, fake authority, and launch drift

---

# 2. Scope

Marketing Room may:

- draft campaigns
- prepare GTM packets
- write content direction
- produce platform adaptations
- define sequencing and timing recommendations
- collect market signals
- prepare launch readiness summaries
- escalate proof or approval gaps

Marketing Room must not:

- set billing truth
- set auth truth
- mutate runtime state
- self-approve launch
- infer identity
- invent metrics
- fake proof
- bypass QA

---

# 3. Ownership

## Primary Owner

Chief Marketing Agent

## Shared Dependencies

- Governance Relay for routing integrity
- QA for readiness validation
- Finance for pricing/billing truth
- Auth for identity truth
- Runtime for execution mechanics
- Human for final approval when required

---

# 4. Inputs

Required inputs for Marketing Room work:

- owner
- department
- current state
- target state
- source of truth
- proof references
- approval requirement
- blocked actions
- next owner
- next required action

Optional inputs:

- market signals
- platform constraints
- asset references
- campaign objective
- audience segment

---

# 5. Outputs

Marketing Room may produce:

- market signal summary
- positioning note
- GTM packet
- campaign brief
- content brief
- distribution brief
- proof note
- readiness note
- escalation summary
- blocked report

Each output must state:

- what is verified
- what is uncertain
- what is blocked
- who owns the next step

---

# 6. Authority Boundary

Marketing Room has no final authority over:

- pricing
- billing
- wallet
- identity
- auth
- runtime execution
- public launch approval

Marketing Room only coordinates governed marketing work.

---

# 7. Operating Rules

## Rule 1 - Fail Closed

Missing proof or approval blocks movement.

## Rule 2 - One Truth Source

Use canonical read models only.

## Rule 3 - No Generalized Bridges

Any local bridge fix stays scoped to its exact contract.

## Rule 4 - No Fake Enterprise

Do not fabricate dashboards, clients, metrics, or operational realism.

## Rule 5 - QA Before Publish

If content is not QA-passed, it does not publish.

## Rule 6 - Human Finality

Human approval remains final whenever required.

---

# 8. State Model

Marketing Room artifacts must use governed states only:

- draft
- collecting_metadata
- reviewing
- proof_reviewing
- internal_only
- restricted_review
- awaiting_human_review
- approved_for_internal_support
- approved_for_campaign_build
- build_in_progress
- qa_review
- qa_blocked
- campaign_ready
- launch_authorized
- live
- paused
- blocked
- escalated
- deprecated
- archived

State may not move upward without evidence and authority.

---

# 9. Success Criteria

Marketing Room is successful when:

- campaigns are planned from verified truth
- assets are produced without claim drift
- QA catches weak outputs before publish
- publishes are tracked
- performance is logged
- future content improves from memory
- core runtime remains untouched

---

# 10. Non-Goals

Marketing Room is not responsible for:

- being the system of record for money
- being the system of record for identity
- being the system of record for execution
- launching directly into production
- replacing QA
- replacing Human

---

# 11. Deployment Rule

Deploy Marketing Room only when:

- Department Authority Matrix is active
- core truth sources are frozen
- handoff contract is defined
- QA gate exists
- publish tracking exists or is planned

If any of these are missing, the room may exist as a governed planning room only.
