# 062_DEPARTMENT_EXECUTION_ROADMAP_v1

**Classification:** `[SYSTEM ROOT]`  
**Status:** Department-specific execution roadmap  
**Purpose:** Define the working order for executing governed department workflows after Master Roadmap v1 is complete.

---

# 0. Roadmap Rule

This roadmap is the execution layer for department work.

It must stay inside the master governance contracts already completed in `061_MASTER_ROADMAP_v1.md`.

If a department-specific flow conflicts with canonical governance, stop and review.

---

# 1. Execution Order

Run department execution in this order:

1. Marketing
2. Media
3. Operations
4. Customer
5. Business Truth
6. Core Governance

---

# 2. Department Responsibilities

## Marketing

- create positioning, GTM framing, brief, and claim boundary
- prepare governed inputs for Media
- never self-launch or mutate truth

## Media

- produce, adapt, package, and QA assets
- keep outputs inside the approved claim boundary
- hand off publish-ready assets to Operations

## Operations

- execute approved flows
- monitor runtime and routing
- report status and route escalations

## Customer

- intake, triage, escalate, and summarize feedback
- keep issue handling outside offer or pricing authority

## Business Truth

- own billing, auth, identity, offer, and package truth
- validate and sanitize read models

## Core Governance

- route, normalize, reconcile, block, and escalate
- protect authority boundaries and final human finality

---

# 3. Required Flow

Use this governed chain as the default:

- Marketing -> Media -> Operations -> Customer -> Business Truth
- Core Governance remains the boundary layer for escalation and reconciliation

## Execution Note

- Marketing execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Media execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Operations execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Customer execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Business Truth execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Core Governance execution surface is complete and read-only.
- Department Execution Roadmap v1 is complete.

---

# 4. Exit Criteria

Department execution is ready when:

- inputs are canonical
- handoffs are explicit
- read surfaces are stable
- blocked states fail closed
- no department invents its own truth or authority

---

# 5. Stop Conditions

Stop and review if:

- a workflow tries to mutate source of truth
- a department bypasses the handoff contract
- a runtime tries to approve itself
- a UI surface introduces write authority
