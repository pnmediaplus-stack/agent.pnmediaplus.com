# KO-09 — Creative & Funnel Architecture v1.0
## Purpose
Convert a bounded campaign hypothesis into executable creative and funnel architecture.

## Inputs
ICP decision, pain wedge, product fit, message/claim boundary, campaign assignment, budget and channel constraints.

## Creative Chain
ICP + Pain + Message + Proof → Angle → Creative Territory → Asset Hypothesis.

## Funnel Chain
Attention → Entry Proposition → Required Action → Qualification Boundary → Next Operational Step.

## Boundaries
- Commercial offer authority is not invented here.
- Lead qualification may be designed as a decision module, but sales handoff remains a downstream operational protocol.
- Entry proposition must be traceable to campaign objective and available authority.

## Output
```yaml
campaign_architecture:
  campaign_objective:
  hypothesis:
  audience:
  creative_territories: []
  angles: []
  proof_plan: []
  channels: []
  entry_proposition:
  conversion_mechanism:
  qualification_rules: []
  operational_boundary:
  budget_constraints:
  risks: []
```

## Acceptance Rule
The Agent must be able to explain why each creative angle exists and which upstream hypothesis it tests.
